## Sequence 7: Detective (Knowledge Keeper)\[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Flordofthemysteries.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FWhite_Tower_Pathway%2FAbilities%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")\]

**Description**<sup id="cite_ref-wechat_reader_abilities_2-4"><a href="https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/White_Tower_Pathway/Abilities#cite_note-wechat_reader_abilities-2" aria-label="Reference 2"><span>[</span>2<span>]</span></a></sup>

A **Detective** is a "profession" that focuses on knowledge acquisition and reasoning ability, along with good fighting and weapon handling skills.

**Traits**<sup id="cite_ref-wechat_reader_abilities_2-5"><a href="https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/White_Tower_Pathway/Abilities#cite_note-wechat_reader_abilities-2" aria-label="Reference 2"><span>[</span>2<span>]</span></a></sup>

-   **Enhanced Mental Attributes**: They can become **Masters** of reasoning.
-   **Weapon Proficiency**: They have become proficient in using weapons.
    -   They can become martial **Masters**; however, this term is relative to ordinary people.
        -   As **Detectives** gain few improvements to their physical attributes, they are not as strong as **Beyonders** who excel in combat.
-   **Physical Enhancement**: **Detectives** have become more agile.

**New Abilities**<sup id="cite_ref-wechat_setting_5_1-2"><a href="https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/White_Tower_Pathway/Abilities#cite_note-wechat_setting_5-1" aria-label="Reference 1"><span>[</span>1<span>]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-wechat_reader_abilities_2-6"><a href="https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/White_Tower_Pathway/Abilities#cite_note-wechat_reader_abilities-2" aria-label="Reference 2"><span>[</span>2<span>]</span></a></sup>

-   **Reconstruction**: **Detectives** can use **Beyonder** methods to analyze and **Reconstruct** events that have occurred within a certain area.

**Strengthened Abilities**

-   **Ritualistic Magic Master**: A **Detective** can become a **Master** when it comes to [Ritualistic Magic](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Ritualistic_Magic "Ritualistic Magic").

## Sequence 6: Polymath\[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Flordofthemysteries.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FWhite_Tower_Pathway%2FAbilities%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")\]

**Description**<sup id="cite_ref-wechat_reader_abilities_2-7"><a href="https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/White_Tower_Pathway/Abilities#cite_note-wechat_reader_abilities-2" aria-label="Reference 2"><span>[</span>2<span>]</span></a></sup>

This **Sequence** allows them to learn and understand a great deal. Because of their extensive knowledge, they become Jack-of-all-trades, able to identify, see clearly, remember, and **Imitate** abilities they've encountered.

**New Abilities**<sup id="cite_ref-wechat_setting_5_1-3"><a href="https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/White_Tower_Pathway/Abilities#cite_note-wechat_setting_5-1" aria-label="Reference 1"><span>[</span>1<span>]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-wechat_reader_abilities_2-8"><a href="https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/White_Tower_Pathway/Abilities#cite_note-wechat_reader_abilities-2" aria-label="Reference 2"><span>[</span>2<span>]</span></a></sup>

-   **Analysis**: With prior knowledge, a **Polymath** can **Analyze** the **Beyonder** powers used by an opponent, quickly identify the specific situation, and understand strengths as well as weaknesses. Of course, **Analysis** can also be wrong and fail.
    -   The more comprehensive their knowledge and the deeper their understanding of the target's [Pathway](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Pathway "Pathway") and **Beyonder** powers, the more effective their **Analysis** becomes.
        -   If the **Polymath** makes errors or an incomplete **Analysis** while dissecting an ability, the ability will mutate strangely or just fail entirely.<sup id="cite_ref-5"><a href="https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/White_Tower_Pathway/Abilities#cite_note-5" aria-label="Reference 5"><span>[</span>5<span>]</span></a></sup>
    -   The prerequisite for a **Polymath's** **Analysis** to work successfully is to have enough preexisting knowledge of the target's [Pathway](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Pathway "Pathway") and subsequent **Beyonder** powers.
    -   **Analysis** let's them rapidly identify a target's **Beyonder** powers, allowing them to know of the target's strengths and weaknesses as well as **Imitate** more quickly.
        -   Despite having only directly confronted [Jason Beria](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Jason_Beria "Jason Beria") once, [Isengard Stanton](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Isengard_Stanton "Isengard Stanton") was able to use [2-081](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/2-081 "2-081") to **Imitate** and utilize the **Beyonder** power **Danger Premonition**.<sup id="cite_ref-1.257_6-0"><a href="https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/White_Tower_Pathway/Abilities#cite_note-1.257-6" aria-label="Reference 6"><span>[</span>6<span>]</span></a></sup>
            -   This case of analyzing a passive ability is attributed to the properties of [2-081](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/2-081 "2-081"), whose formation method enhanced the power of **Analysis**.<sup id="cite_ref-7"><a href="https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/White_Tower_Pathway/Abilities#cite_note-7" aria-label="Reference 7"><span>[</span>7<span>]</span></a></sup>
    -   They can **Analyze** **Demigod**\-level **Beyonder** powers.<sup id="cite_ref-wechat_mod_q36_8-0"><a href="https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/White_Tower_Pathway/Abilities#cite_note-wechat_mod_q36-8" aria-label="Reference 8"><span>[</span>8<span>]</span></a></sup>
    -   They can **Analyze** **Fate**\-related **Beyonder** powers.<sup id="cite_ref-wechat_visionary_formulas_q42_9-0"><a href="https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/White_Tower_Pathway/Abilities#cite_note-wechat_visionary_formulas_q42-9" aria-label="Reference 9"><span>[</span>9<span>]</span></a></sup>

-   **Imitation**: Once sufficiently **Analyzed**, There is a chance for the **Polymath** to **Imitate** an **Analyzed** ability to a certain extent.
    -   The more effective their **Analysis** is, the higher the probability that their **Imitation** succeeds.<sup id="cite_ref-wechat_setting_5_1-4"><a href="https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/White_Tower_Pathway/Abilities#cite_note-wechat_setting_5-1" aria-label="Reference 1"><span>[</span>1<span>]</span></a></sup> However, the higher the [Sequence](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sequence "Sequence") of the **Beyonder** power, the higher the chances of failure.<sup id="cite_ref-1.257_6-1"><a href="https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/White_Tower_Pathway/Abilities#cite_note-1.257-6" aria-label="Reference 6"><span>[</span>6<span>]</span></a></sup>
        -   Even if a **Polymath** manages to **Imitate** a **Beyonder** power of the same [Sequence](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sequence "Sequence") level as them, the power they **Imitate** will always be very different from the original.
            -   [Edwina Edwards](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Edwina_Edwards "Edwina Edwards") failed in her search for messengers partly because the **Imitation** of undead powers weren't enough to sign a **Contract**.<sup id="cite_ref-10"><a href="https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/White_Tower_Pathway/Abilities#cite_note-10" aria-label="Reference 10"><span>[</span>10<span>]</span></a></sup>
    -   They can **Imitate** passive **Beyonder** powers such as a **[Devil](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Abyss_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_6:_Devil)'s** **Danger Premonition**. However, such abilities cannot be copied through observation alone and requires anatomical research.<sup id="cite_ref-11"><a href="https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/White_Tower_Pathway/Abilities#cite_note-11" aria-label="Reference 11"><span>[</span>11<span>]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-12"><a href="https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/White_Tower_Pathway/Abilities#cite_note-12" aria-label="Reference 12"><span>[</span>12<span>]</span></a></sup>
        -   They can **Imitate** even powers like **Recording**, but it requires a lot of knowledge.<sup id="cite_ref-13"><a href="https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/White_Tower_Pathway/Abilities#cite_note-13" aria-label="Reference 13"><span>[</span>13<span>]</span></a></sup>
        -   **Polymaths** are also able to **Imitate** **Fate**\-related **Beyonder** powers.<sup id="cite_ref-wechat_visionary_formulas_q42_9-1"><a href="https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/White_Tower_Pathway/Abilities#cite_note-wechat_visionary_formulas_q42-9" aria-label="Reference 9"><span>[</span>9<span>]</span></a></sup>
    -   At the stage of a **Polymath**, as long as the corresponding knowledge is **Analyzed** and mastered, there is no limit to the number of **Spells** and abilities that can be **Imitated**.
        -   However, the effect of **Imitation** will vary depending on the degree of **Analysis**, the degree of knowledge mastery in addition to the degree of potion digestion.
            -   It may be _20%_ at the worst, and can reach to _50%_ of the original at the best.
    -   They can **Imitate** powers at the **Demigod**\-level, and if the knowledge is fully grasped, its effects are not much different from a **[Scribe](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Door_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_6:_Scribe)'s** **Recording**.<sup id="cite_ref-wechat_mod_q36_8-1"><a href="https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/White_Tower_Pathway/Abilities#cite_note-wechat_mod_q36-8" aria-label="Reference 8"><span>[</span>8<span>]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-14"><a href="https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/White_Tower_Pathway/Abilities#cite_note-14"><span>[</span>Imitation 1<span>]</span></a></sup>
        -   The chances of successfully **Imitating** a **Demigod**\-level power is very low, and they must fully master the knowledge of the corresponding power to reach its maximum level.
        -   Such **Imitations** can harm an **[Overseer](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Chaos_Mist_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_4:_Overseer)** who is immune to attacks that lack godhood.<sup id="cite_ref-wechat_mod_q36_8-2"><a href="https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/White_Tower_Pathway/Abilities#cite_note-wechat_mod_q36-8" aria-label="Reference 8"><span>[</span>8<span>]</span></a></sup>
    -   This is different compared to **Replication/Recording** of the [![Door Symbol2](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/lord-of-the-mystery/images/e/e6/Door_Symbol2.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/26?cb=20260330060757)](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Door_Pathway) [Door Pathway](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Door_Pathway).
        -   Using a computer analogy, **Imitation** is equivalent to reverse-engineering the code yourself and writing a similar one, whereas **Recording** and **Replication** are equivalent to a "Copy" operation.<sup id="cite_ref-15"><a href="https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/White_Tower_Pathway/Abilities#cite_note-15" aria-label="Reference 14"><span>[</span>14<span>]</span></a></sup>
    -   **Imitation** can't **Imitate** authorities, even when one reaches **Sequence 0**.<sup id="cite_ref-16"><a href="https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/White_Tower_Pathway/Abilities#cite_note-16" aria-label="Reference 15"><span>[</span>15<span>]</span></a></sup>
        -   The abilities within an authority can still be **Imitated**, but they are inferior to the permission itself, meaning they will be suppressed by the permission.<sup id="cite_ref-17"><a href="https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/White_Tower_Pathway/Abilities#cite_note-17" aria-label="Reference 16"><span>[</span>16<span>]</span></a></sup>

## Sequence 9: Reader\[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Flordofthemysteries.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FWhite_Tower_Pathway%2FAbilities%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")\]

-   **Mystic Arts**: A **Mysticism Magister** masters various magical, diverse, unique, and rare **Mystic Arts**, and can even invent their own strange yet effective **Spells**. **Mystic Arts** are formed from the **Analysis** of **Principles**.<sup id="cite_ref-:1_18-0"><a href="https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/White_Tower_Pathway/Abilities#cite_note-:1-18" aria-label="Reference 17"><span>[</span>17<span>]</span></a></sup>
    -   Depending on the chosen [Advancement Ritual](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/White_Tower_Pathway/Advancement#Sequence_5:_Mysticism_Magister), the area of the **Mystic Arts** created and mastered by the **Mysticism** **Magister** will vary:
        -   "Teaching at least five **Mid-Sequence** **Beyonders** who have independently comprehended [Acting Method](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Acting_Method "Acting Method")" means being more adept at creating and using **Mystic Arts** in the corresponding **Mid-Sequence Beyonder's** field.
        -   "Teaching at least fifty Erudites" means being well rounded in the **Mystic Arts** field, with no weaknesses but also no strengths.
    -   When a **Mysticism Magister** first advances, they can naturally obtain _6 to_ _13 **Mystic Arts**_ from the current **Omniscience** authority holder.
        -   After that, they can acquire **Mystic Arts** through learning and self-creation.
    -   **Mysticism Magisters** can achieve the effect of being well-rounded with this ability.
        -   So long as they have the required knowledge and materials at hand, they can make any **Spell** from healing to defense, offense, and support as **Omniscience** implies **Omnipotence**.<sup id="cite_ref-19"><a href="https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/White_Tower_Pathway/Abilities#cite_note-19" aria-label="Reference 18"><span>[</span>18<span>]</span></a></sup>
    -   Their **Spells** include definitions such as **Uncommon**, **Unique to Oneself**, and **Distinctive**, and at the same time, their **Spells** may or may not have casting steps.<sup id="cite_ref-20"><a href="https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/White_Tower_Pathway/Abilities#cite_note-20" aria-label="Reference 19"><span>[</span>19<span>]</span></a></sup>
        -   Unlike the **Spells** created by **[Warlocks](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Hermit_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_7:_Warlock)**, **Mystic Arts** can often omit the casting materials, incantations, and most casting steps, making them faster and more secretive.
            -   Of course, not all **Mystic Arts** can omit those. **Mystic Arts** with prior preparations generally either have very special effects or are very powerful.
        -   By obtaining a weakened version of an ability and strengthening it through continuous **Analysis**, they can develop unique new skills through independent refinement, fusion, and deduction.<sup id="cite_ref-21"><a href="https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/White_Tower_Pathway/Abilities#cite_note-21" aria-label="Reference 20"><span>[</span>20<span>]</span></a></sup>
    -   Some of the **Spells** and techniques they can use include but are not limited to:
        -   **Vile Thoughts**:<sup>⁂</sup> As [Edwina Edwards](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Edwina_Edwards "Edwina Edwards") ’s light blue eyes turned black again, sticky vile thoughts slowly flowed. She suddenly clenched her right palm, causing [Ulyssan](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Ulyssan "Ulyssan") to raise its head in a roar. It was in excruciating pain as light blue liquid flowed from the corner of its eyes and mouth, causing emotional disturbance on its [Spirit Body](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Soul "Soul").<sup id="cite_ref-:2_22-0"><a href="https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/White_Tower_Pathway/Abilities#cite_note-:2-22" aria-label="Reference 21"><span>[</span>21<span>]</span></a></sup>
        -   **Evil Spell**:<sup>⁂</sup> [Edwina Edwards](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Edwina_Edwards "Edwina Edwards") made her light-blue eyes turn black. Inside them flowed a sticky liquid that seemed to be the manifestation of all the evil in the depths of one’s heart. She gently clenched her right palm as [Ulyssan](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Ulyssan "Ulyssan") stood up with a roar, instantly ripping apart the effects of Imprison.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_23-0"><a href="https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/White_Tower_Pathway/Abilities#cite_note-:0-23" aria-label="Reference 22"><span>[</span>22<span>]</span></a></sup>
            -   The [frost dragon](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Ulyssan "Ulyssan")’s eerie-blue eyes were filled with blankness and pain. It seemed to be still immersed in the emotions of sudden madness and ruthlessness. Even though this was a state it was always in, to have it fully triggered was uncomfortable.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_23-1"><a href="https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/White_Tower_Pathway/Abilities#cite_note-:0-23" aria-label="Reference 22"><span>[</span>22<span>]</span></a></sup>
        -   **Mirror Curse**: [Edwina Edwards](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Edwina_Edwards "Edwina Edwards") used a bizarre but powerful spell that uses a brass mirror to reflect [Ulyssan](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Ulyssan "Ulyssan")'s body parts, pinching the reflection at the mirror’s surface before pulling outward, causing **Ulyssan'**s wound to instantly worsen.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_23-2"><a href="https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/White_Tower_Pathway/Abilities#cite_note-:0-23" aria-label="Reference 22"><span>[</span>22<span>]</span></a></sup>

            -   **Evans Freezing Ray**: This is a high-precision **Freezing Spell** that emits a concentrated, colorless dim ray that **Freezes** specific targets, turning their skin into a layer of crystal **Ice** and also **Freezing** the target's surroundings.
            -   **Elemental Order**: This is a **Spell** based on the periodic law of basic elements, capable of precisely arranging electrons, allowing the them to _control and combine_ elements for attack, defense or recycling.
        -   **Mysticism Magisters** can use **Mystic Arts** to delay the loss of control of **Beyonders**, or to permanently or temporarily enhance their own physical abilities.

**Strengthened Abilities**<sup id="cite_ref-wechat_reader_abilities_2-10"><a href="https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/White_Tower_Pathway/Abilities#cite_note-wechat_reader_abilities-2" aria-label="Reference 2"><span>[</span>2<span>]</span></a></sup>

-   The previous **Sequences**’ abilities have been enhanced.
    -   **Imitation**: Their **Imitated** **Spells** can now have _60%_ of the original effect.

TRANSLATION NOTE

In the original Chinese text, 规律 (guīlǜ) carries multiple meanings and in this context, can be translated as "**Laws**", as is done in the English WebNovel translation. However, as the term "**Law**" is already used for a similar word, 律法 (lǜfǎ), and both are treated as distinct authorities, this page will consistently use a single English term (**Principles**) to represent it for clarity and distinction.

-   **Laws** (律法, _lǜfǎ_):
    -   Refers to codified, enforceable laws—rules established by authority, often carrying judicial or binding force. It aligns with legal systems, decrees, and formal structures of governance.
-   **Principles** (规律 _guīlǜ_):
    -   Refers to inherent patterns or natural laws—the underlying order of reality that exists independently of authority. It is closer to universal principles or the fundamental workings of the world.
    -   In other contexts this term can also denote simpler patterns or regularity, not a strict law, but rather an observable consistency or recurring behavior over time such as everyday routines or behavioral patterns.

**Authorities**<sup id="cite_ref-wechat_setting_5_1-8"><a href="https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/White_Tower_Pathway/Abilities#cite_note-wechat_setting_5-1" aria-label="Reference 1"><span>[</span>1<span>]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-wechat_reader_abilities_2-15"><a href="https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/White_Tower_Pathway/Abilities#cite_note-wechat_reader_abilities-2" aria-label="Reference 2"><span>[</span>2<span>]</span></a></sup>

    -   **Cognizing**: It is the ability to understand the rules of the world and grasp many profound mysteries, enemies standing in front of a **Cognizer** can rarely hide any secret. Everything, be it their strengths, weakness, or anything else is all laid bare for the **Cognizer** to see.
        -   They **Cognize** the essence without any senses or specific forms of cognition, effectively viewing the true essence of things without relying on their senses.<sup id="cite_ref-37"><a href="https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/White_Tower_Pathway/Abilities#cite_note-37" aria-label="Reference 35"><span>[</span>35<span>]</span></a></sup>
            -   They can use their deep insight to see through and **Analyze** certain symbols and use it to create **Spells** through a process of **Imitation**.
                -   For example, **Cognizers** can look and peer into the symbols of [Lumian's Dream](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Lumian%27s_Dream "Lumian's Dream") and derive certain **Spells** based off the symbols they have comprehended.<sup id="cite_ref-38"><a href="https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/White_Tower_Pathway/Abilities#cite_note-38" aria-label="Reference 36"><span>[</span>36<span>]</span></a></sup>
