## Sequence 9: Corpse Collector\[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Flordofthemysteries.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FDeath_Pathway%2FAbilities%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")\]

**Description**<sup id="cite_ref-wechat_death_abilities_1-0"><a href="https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Death_Pathway/Abilities#cite_note-wechat_death_abilities-1" aria-label="Reference 1"><span>[</span>1<span>]</span></a></sup>

From this stage forward, these **Beyonders** will become increasingly afraid of **Sunlight** and **Purification**.

**Traits**

-   **Physical Enhancement**: A **Corpse Collector**, upon drinking the potion, will not only gain enhanced physical attributes but will also develop a gloomy presence and a lower body temperature.
    -   A **Corpse Collector** will gain resistance to the **Cold**, **Decay**, and **Corrosiveness** of cadaveric auras, making them resistant to such effects to a certain extent.
    -   Their gloomy presence and lower body temperature will prevent a **Corpse Collector** from being attacked by **Undead** creatures and **Spirits**.

-   **Spirituality**: Their **Spirituality** is slightly enhanced as a **Beyonder**, allowing them to use **Spirit Vision**.
    -   **Spirit Vision**: A **Corpse Collector** can use **Spirit Vision** to see non-physical things, such as ghosts and specters. They could see the different parts of a [Soul](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Soul "Soul"), deduce a person's health and emotions, and determine if something has a magical aura.
        -   Due to their unique nature, a **Corpse Collector** is able to directly see spiritual bodies, including a portion of [Evil Spirits](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Evil_Spirits "Evil Spirits") and restless **Wraiths**, even without activating their **Spirit Vision**.

**New Abilities**<sup id="cite_ref-2"><a href="https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Death_Pathway/Abilities#cite_note-2" aria-label="Reference 2"><span>[</span>2<span>]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-1.918_3-0"><a href="https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Death_Pathway/Abilities#cite_note-1.918-3" aria-label="Reference 3"><span>[</span>3<span>]</span></a></sup>

-   **Knowledge** (**Undead**): **Corpse Collectors** will gain an abundant wealth of information pertaining to the specific characteristics and weaknesses of many **Undead** creatures.
    -   This also includes just regular corpses which makes a **Corpse Collector** an expert on autopsy.

## Sequence 7: Spirit Medium\[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Flordofthemysteries.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FDeath_Pathway%2FAbilities%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")\]

**Traits**

-   **Physical Enhancements**: Compared to the previous two **Sequences**, the **Spirit Medium** does not gain significant improvements in physique or agility.
-   **Spirituality**: A **Spirit Medium's Spirituality** has been greatly enhanced.
    -   **Spiritual Perception**: Their **Spiritual Perception** becomes extremely strong.
        -   They can sense the hidden **Spirits** around them even with their eyes closed.
    -   **Spirit Vision**: Their **Spirit Vision** becomes more powerful.
-   **Spirit Affinity**:<sup>⁂</sup> To the deceased and natural **Spirits** around them, the **Spirit Medium** is akin to that of a fellow **Spirit** and thus not dangerous to them.
    -   If the **Spirit Medium** is friendly enough, those deceased and natural **Spirits** will even take the initiative to pass on information to them.

**New Abilities**<sup id="cite_ref-wechat_death_abilities_1-3"><a href="https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Death_Pathway/Abilities#cite_note-wechat_death_abilities-1" aria-label="Reference 1"><span>[</span>1<span>]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-1.918_3-3"><a href="https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Death_Pathway/Abilities#cite_note-1.918-3" aria-label="Reference 3"><span>[</span>3<span>]</span></a></sup>

-   **Spirit Channeling**: Their core ability is **Spirit Channeling**, allowing them to directly communicate with the natural **Spirits** and loitering dead souls in the real world.
    -   With the advancement of each **Sequence**, the quantity and quality of natural **Spirits**, **Undead** and [Spirit World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Spirit_World "Spirit World") creatures they can control and order increases exponentially.
    -   They can use different **Spirits** to actualize different kinds of effects, creating various kinds of supernatural phenomena in a rather multifaceted way.
        -   Theoretically, a **Spirit Channeling** done by a **Spirit Medium** can involve only _1 **Spirit**_.<sup id="cite_ref-wechat_fate_rituals_q72_6-0"><a href="https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Death_Pathway/Abilities#cite_note-wechat_fate_rituals_q72-6" aria-label="Reference 6"><span>[</span>6<span>]</span></a></sup>
        -   Most **Spirits** need corresponding materials to safely communicate with.
            -   These ingredients include but are not limited to **Full Moon Essence Oil**, **Corpse Incense Medication**, as well as a **Spirit Medium's** own blood.<sup id="cite_ref-2.679_7-0"><a href="https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Death_Pathway/Abilities#cite_note-2.679-7" aria-label="Reference 7"><span>[</span>7<span>]</span></a></sup>
        -   Some examples of the **Spirits** they have been shown to use are:
            -   **Illusory Eye**:<sup>⁂</sup> A **Spirit World Creature** with a nearly transparent eye and ghastly-pale eye whites, looking down from above without blinking.<sup id="cite_ref-1.658_8-0"><a href="https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Death_Pathway/Abilities#cite_note-1.658-8" aria-label="Reference 8"><span>[</span>8<span>]</span></a></sup>
                -   It allows them to scout areas from afar, a **Spirit Medium’s** version of a telescope.
            -   **Frost Shadow**:<sup>⁂</sup> A **Spirit** that can create a considerable **Frozen** domain within a _10 to 20 meter range_, and can materialize a layer of armor-like **Ice** and a colossal, sharp, and crystalline **Frost** scythe.<sup id="cite_ref-2.679_7-1"><a href="https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Death_Pathway/Abilities#cite_note-2.679-7" aria-label="Reference 7"><span>[</span>7<span>]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-9"><a href="https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Death_Pathway/Abilities#cite_note-9" aria-label="Reference 9"><span>[</span>9<span>]</span></a></sup>
                -   This is among the most powerful **Spirits** they can **Channel** without the use of materials.
            -   **Earth Spirit**:<sup>⁂</sup> A **Natural Spirit**<sup id="cite_ref-10"><a href="https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Death_Pathway/Abilities#cite_note-10" aria-label="Reference 10"><span>[</span>10<span>]</span></a></sup> with a pair of weathered, stone-like hands, it can be used to soften the ground like a swamp and drag a target underground, like a **[Druid](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mother_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_5:_Druid)'s Underground Slink**.<sup id="cite_ref-2.679_7-2"><a href="https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Death_Pathway/Abilities#cite_note-2.679-7" aria-label="Reference 7"><span>[</span>7<span>]</span></a></sup>
                -   This **Spirit** needs their blood to be **Channeled**.
    -   Since they can directly communicate with natural **Spirits** and ghosts, they have informants everywhere that can alert them of any danger or information.<sup id="cite_ref-1.914_11-0"><a href="https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Death_Pathway/Abilities#cite_note-1.914-11" aria-label="Reference 11"><span>[</span>11<span>]</span></a></sup>
    -   It can also be used on living creatures to directly communicate with their **Spirits** to obtain real information, but this method holds a lot of danger for them.
        -   **Channeling** a living [Soul](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Soul "Soul") is difficult due to that [Soul](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Soul "Soul") possessing its own will; however, this could be faced by a **Spirit Medium** with two methods.
            1.  **Spirit Mediums** can use their powerful **Spirituality** to triumph over the will of the other person, engaging in a barbaric method of communication.
            2.  They can use medication to make the other party relax. Some examples are using **Amantha Essence** and **Eye of the Spirit** medication.
        -   They have the means to protect themselves under the risk of being infected by a living **Beyonder's** potent **Spirituality**, which can't be learned or used by **Beyonders** of other [Pathways](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Pathways "Pathways"). This is the case even when those means are explained to them.<sup id="cite_ref-1.183_12-0"><a href="https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Death_Pathway/Abilities#cite_note-1.183-12" aria-label="Reference 12"><span>[</span>12<span>]</span></a></sup>
    -   A **Spirit Medium** is able to directly **Channel** recently deceased **Spirits**, such as fallen enemies, in order to obtain even more information from them.<sup id="cite_ref-13"><a href="https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Death_Pathway/Abilities#cite_note-13" aria-label="Reference 13"><span>[</span>13<span>]</span></a></sup>
    -   A **Spirit Medium** could communicate with surrounding **Spirits** or through specific directions and descriptions without needing [Ritualistic Magic](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Ritualistic_Magic "Ritualistic Magic").<sup id="cite_ref-14"><a href="https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Death_Pathway/Abilities#cite_note-14" aria-label="Reference 14"><span>[</span>14<span>]</span></a></sup>
        -   However, unlike a **[Dancer](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Eternal_Aeon_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_9:_Dancer)**, they cannot rely on a **Summoning Dance** and a location's uniqueness to attract more distant, hidden **Spirits**.

-   **Zombie Disguise**:<sup>⁂</sup> They can also **Disguise** themselves as a **Zombie** in order to better endure the erosion of **Decay**, **Cold**, **Death** and other auras.

**Strengthened Abilities**<sup id="cite_ref-wechat_death_abilities_1-4"><a href="https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Death_Pathway/Abilities#cite_note-wechat_death_abilities-1" aria-label="Reference 1"><span>[</span>1<span>]</span></a></sup>

-   **Knowledge** (**Mysticism**): They gain knowledge of various kinds of [Ritualistic Magic](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Ritualistic_Magic "Ritualistic Magic") related to **Spirits**.
    -   They are armed with knowledge on how to deal with the dangers that come with **Channeling** a living creature's **Spirit**, as well as the effective communication methods in such a state.<sup id="cite_ref-1.183_12-1"><a href="https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Death_Pathway/Abilities#cite_note-1.183-12" aria-label="Reference 12"><span>[</span>12<span>]</span></a></sup>

-   **Eye of Death**: This **Beyonder** power becomes more powerful.

## Sequence 6: Spirit Guide\[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Flordofthemysteries.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FDeath_Pathway%2FAbilities%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")\]

**Description**

It is clear that **Spirit Guides** are very powerful against **Zombies** and **Wraiths**, and can even effectively target [Evil Spirits](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Evil_Spirits "Evil Spirits"). They can directly control groups of ownerless **Undead** that are of similar status to themselves; however, when facing **Higher Sequence Beyonders** of the [![Death Symbol2](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/lord-of-the-mystery/images/b/b2/Death_Symbol2.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/26?cb=20260330060733)](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Death_Pathway) [Death Pathway](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Death_Pathway), their slaves are very prone to rebelling, and so are they themselves.

**New Abilities**<sup id="cite_ref-wechat_death_abilities_1-5"><a href="https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Death_Pathway/Abilities#cite_note-wechat_death_abilities-1" aria-label="Reference 1"><span>[</span>1<span>]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-1.918_3-4"><a href="https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Death_Pathway/Abilities#cite_note-1.918-3" aria-label="Reference 3"><span>[</span>3<span>]</span></a></sup>

-   **Language of the Dead**: By speaking a mystical language, they can urge a target's **Spirit** to leave a body, bypassing the physical protection provided by flesh and blood to target the **Spirit Body**.<sup id="cite_ref-1.918_3-5"><a href="https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Death_Pathway/Abilities#cite_note-1.918-3" aria-label="Reference 3"><span>[</span>3<span>]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-1.660_15-0"><a href="https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Death_Pathway/Abilities#cite_note-1.660-15" aria-label="Reference 15"><span>[</span>15<span>]</span></a></sup>
    -   This is an advancement of a **Spirit Medium's** powers. It could go from direct communication with the **Spirit Body** to that of commandeering and **Enslavement**.<sup id="cite_ref-1.660_15-1"><a href="https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Death_Pathway/Abilities#cite_note-1.660-15" aria-label="Reference 15"><span>[</span>15<span>]</span></a></sup>
        -   As **Marionettes** are considered dead, a **Spirit Guide** can interfere and influence a **Marionette**; however, at **Sequence 4**, the core of a **Marionette** can have a **Worm of Spirit** inside. Thus, at those stages it also depends on multiple factors like relative strength of both sides, condition, how much effort is exerted, etc.<sup id="cite_ref-16"><a href="https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Death_Pathway/Abilities#cite_note-16" aria-label="Reference 16"><span>[</span>16<span>]</span></a></sup>

-   **Resurrection**: They can turn corpses into living skeletons and **Zombies**; however, this kind of **Resurrection** does not allow the reanimated corpse to have any will or vitality.<sup id="cite_ref-17"><a href="https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Death_Pathway/Abilities#cite_note-17" aria-label="Reference 17"><span>[</span>17<span>]</span></a></sup>

**Strengthened Abilities**<sup id="cite_ref-wechat_death_abilities_1-6"><a href="https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Death_Pathway/Abilities#cite_note-wechat_death_abilities-1" aria-label="Reference 1"><span>[</span>1<span>]</span></a></sup>

-   **Spirit Channeling**: They can influence and are involved in the [Spirit World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Spirit_World "Spirit World"), able to recruit messengers on their own, as well as obtain help from certain [Spirit World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Spirit_World "Spirit World") creatures.

        -   However, making these deceased and natural **Spirits** always follow their commands without being discovered and affected by other factors is a significant problem for a **Spirit Guide**.
        -   In comparison with a **Spirit Medium's** capabilities, **Spirit Guides** are now capable of controlling groups of ownerless **Undead** that are of similar status to themselves.
            -   The upper limit of **Undead** and **Spirits** they can control numbers in the thousands.<sup id="cite_ref-wechat_fate_rituals_q72_6-1"><a href="https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Death_Pathway/Abilities#cite_note-wechat_fate_rituals_q72-6" aria-label="Reference 6"><span>[</span>6<span>]</span></a></sup>

    -   Some examples of the creatures that have been shown to be under their command are:

An **Undead** creature specialized in consuming shadows and shadowy creatures.<sup id="cite_ref-2.523_20-0"><a href="https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Death_Pathway/Abilities#cite_note-2.523-20" aria-label="Reference 20"><span>[</span>20<span>]</span></a></sup>

## Sequence 5: Gatekeeper\[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Flordofthemysteries.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FDeath_Pathway%2FAbilities%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D5&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")\]

**Traits**

-   **Physical Enhancement**: They become stronger and quicker, one step can allow them to suddenly close the distance against opponents and thrust at lightning speed, bringing vigorous wind currents.<sup id="cite_ref-1.660_15-2"><a href="https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Death_Pathway/Abilities#cite_note-1.660-15" aria-label="Reference 15"><span>[</span>15<span>]</span></a></sup>
-   **Spiritual Perception**: They can acutely sense if an unknown creature is crossing the [Spirit World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Spirit_World "Spirit World") and approaching a place near them.<sup id="cite_ref-24"><a href="https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Death_Pathway/Abilities#cite_note-24" aria-label="Reference 24"><span>[</span>24<span>]</span></a></sup>

On each feather, illusory black tubes extend outward, and each scale and feather bears strange symbols of different shapes.<sup id="cite_ref-wechat_death_formulas_41-1"><a href="https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Death_Pathway/Abilities#cite_note-wechat_death_formulas-41" aria-label="Reference 41"><span>[</span>41<span>]</span></a></sup>
